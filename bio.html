My Bio

My Background

Kori Bui

Keep • On • Going

Hi, I’m Kori! My journey started in Ames, Iowa, took a detour through Pennsylvania, and finally landed me in sunny California.

How did I get my name? I was actually expected to arrive while my parents were in South Korea. To avoid any international copyright infringement, they legally dropped the "a"—and thus, Kori was born.

What I Do

  • Athletics: I’m a dedicated competitive volleyball player. When I’m not dominating the court for my school team, you can find me diving for saves in the sand. Beach volleyball has my whole heart (and most of the sand in my shoes).
  • Customer Experience: By day, I hold things down at Target, mastering the art of guest services and logistics. If you need to know which aisle has the best items, I'm your person.
  • Social & Style: When the jersey comes off and the shift ends, I’m usually out shopping, exploring new style trends, or hanging out with my friends.

Fueled By Genius (And Heavy Carbs)

Every great athlete and retail professional needs a rigorous training diet. Mine is strictly curated around premium gastronomy, including:

  • The Papa Johns Special: Mushroom and bacon pizza, completely submerged in garlic sauce.
  • The Ice Cream Peak: Ben & Jerry’s Half Baked, or—strictly speaking—just the crunchy top layer of a Dirt Cake pint.
  • The Gourmet Dessert: Crumbl’s Dubai Chocolate Brownie (a culinary masterpiece).
  • The Target Power Lunch: A tactical combination of Rainbow Goldfish and Applesauce Crushers.
